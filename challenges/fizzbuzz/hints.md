# FizzBuzz hints

Reveal only one level at a time.

## Hint 1

Consider every number independently. Determine what must be appended to the
result before advancing to the next number.

## Hint 2

One category is more specific than the others because it satisfies two rules
at once. Handle the most specific case before the individual cases.

## Hint 3

Use the remainder operator to test divisibility. A value divisible by both 3
and 5 has remainder zero for both operations.
